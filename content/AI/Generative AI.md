
### Introduction

Generative AI refers to a class of artificial intelligence models that can generate new content on the lines of below
- text
- audio
- video
- code
- images
which resembles human created data.

#### Generative AI Model Examples
- Text -  **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, **Grok**
- Image - **Dalle**, **Midjourney**
- Code - **CodeLLama**
- TTS (Text to Speech) - **Eleven Labs**
- Video - **Sora**

### Generative AI vs Traditional AI
- Traditional AI or Classical ML, DL as we can call it - refers to pre Gen AI era.
- It finds patterns in data and provides predictions.
- Gen AI is all about learning the distribution of data, so that a new sample can be generated from it.

### Generative AI vs Agentic AI
- Gen AI is all about creating content, where as Agentic AI is all about solving/achieving a goal.
- Gen AI is reactive, where as Agentic AI is proactive and autonomous.
- Gen AI is a building block of Agentic AI
	- Agentic AI has a series of steps - Reasoning, Memory, Generation, Context Awareness, Adaptability etc etc.
- Gen AI can be considered as a subset of Agentic AI
